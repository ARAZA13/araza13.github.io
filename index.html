Selected Projects & Publications

Global Education Policy Dashboard (GEPD)

Provided strategic and technical leadership for the World Bank’s flagship multi-country innovation platform implemented in Peru, Ethiopia, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Central African Republic, and Pakistan. Combined multiple data initiatives to give a systematic overview of the education sector and identify country-specific investment and reform priorities.

State of Global Learning Poverty — 2022 Update

Lead architect of the core analytical framework and simulation model estimating COVID-19 impact on global learning poverty. Developed and maintained core components of the GLAD and Learning Poverty repositories. The indicator directly informs World Bank operational strategies aimed at halving Learning Poverty by 2030.

Pakistan – Human Capital Review

Led the econometric analysis for the World Bank’s flagship country report, applying Mincerian wage regressions, Probit models, and Heckman selection corrections to quantify returns to education and the role of cognitive and socioemotional skills. Designed visualizations that informed national policy on human capital development and economic inclusion.

Data Depot Pakistan

Devised and implemented a scalable data cleaning, harmonization, and analysis strategy for 100+ complex micro-datasets. Built proprietary panels of point estimates and a scalable R Shiny application for indicator tracking. All code is open-source on GitHub.

Learning and Educational Achievement in Pakistan Schools (LEAPS)

Contributed to the latest round of one of the largest education research programs in any low-income country. Supported student tracking, questionnaire design for cognitive and non-cognitive skills, team building, and research design. Also worked on the education financing RCT that produced an NBER working paper.

State Authority in Pakistan

Developed and trained the first in-house field team at CERP and contributed to research design and analysis for the paper “Trust in State and Non-State Actors: Evidence from Dispute Resolution in Pakistan” (Journal of Political Economy, 2020). Acted as primary liaison between survey teams, government institutions, and tech companies.
